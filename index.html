Log in with Spotify to search songs and create playlists directly from your browser. Your credentials are handled securely by Spotify — this app never sees your password.

⚠ Why use your own Client ID?
The default Spotify App runs in development mode, which is limited to 25 users. If you can't log in (getting a 403 error), the limit has been reached. Creating your own Spotify App takes 2 minutes and gives you your own quota.

Follow these steps to create your own Spotify App:

  1. Go to the Spotify Developer Dashboard and log in with your Spotify account.
  2. Click "Create app". Give it any name (e.g. "My Playlist Creator") and description.
  3. In the "Redirect URI" field, paste this exact URL:
  4. Under "Which API/SDKs are you planning to use?", select Web API.
  5. Click "Save".
  6. Go to "Settings" on your new app. Copy the Client ID (it looks like a 32-character hex string).
  7. Paste it below and click "Save Client ID".
Leave empty to use the default app.
💡 Is this safe? The Client ID is not a secret — it's designed to be public in Spotify's PKCE authentication flow. Your password and tokens are handled entirely by Spotify. This page never stores or transmits anything sensitive.
